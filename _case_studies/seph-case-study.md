## Overview
Consolidated three regional public health websites into a single platform to serve residents reliably, improve accessibility, and reduce operational duplication.

## Challenge
Fragmented outreach and duplicated content across legacy sites made it hard for residents to find critical health information.

## Discovery & Planning
- Content audit across 2,000+ pages
- Information architecture to simplify navigation
- Stakeholder workshops to align priorities and bilingual needs

## Solution
Built a modern WordPress ecosystem with:
- Unified navigation and platform
- Centralized CMS for easier content updates
- Accessible design meeting WCAG guidelines
- Bilingual (EN/FR) content support

## Results
- 1 Platform: Single source for community access
- 0 Duplication: Reduced redundant pages and maintenance
- 40% Faster: Faster task completion and content updates
- 100% Scalable: Modular architecture for future growth

## Takeaways
Prioritizing IA, accessibility, and a centralized CMS delivered measurable outcomes and improved user trust.
