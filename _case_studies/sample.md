## Challenge
The existing widget UI confused users and lowered conversion.

## Approach
Conducted user interviews, created prototypes, and iterated with A/B tests.

## Results
- 24% increase in engagement
- 12% lift in conversions

## Takeaways
Design focused on clarity and progressive disclosure drove measurable impact.
